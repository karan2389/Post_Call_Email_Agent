# Customer consent and communication policy checklist (C0, D-068)

Purpose: list what the **business** must confirm before any post-call follow-up reaches a customer. Nothing here is assumed and **no customer is selected**. This is a checklist, not legal advice; the owner should involve legal or the data-protection lead where noted. **Status of every item: NOT CONFIRMED.**

| # | Confirm | Why | Owner | Evidence to record | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The legal basis for sending a follow-up email after a support call, and that this is a service follow-up, not marketing | Existing customers do not automatically consent to every type of communication | Business owner + legal | Written decision, date, who | Open |
| 2 | The jurisdictions customers are in and which rules apply (for example India DPDP, EU/UK GDPR and ePrivacy, US CAN-SPAM, Canada CASL) | Rules differ for service versus promotional email | Legal | Short written note | Open |
| 3 | Customers are told on the call that it is recorded, and the privacy notice covers recording and sharing a recording link | The email shares a Fathom recording | Business owner / support lead | Notice text and where it is shown | Open |
| 4 | Who can open a Fathom link (public to anyone with the link, or restricted), and whether recordings contain other people's data | A wrapped or forwarded link may reach third parties | Support lead | Fathom sharing setting screenshot or note | Open |
| 5 | The company's existing opt-out / do-not-contact lists and how they are matched against recipients | A customer who opted out must never receive these emails | Business owner | Name and location of each list, owner | Open |
| 6 | How a customer opts out of these follow-ups (reply "stop") and who processes it, within what time | The emails carry no unsubscribe link (the Gmail connector cannot add one) | Support lead | Written procedure | Open |
| 7 | Whether the email must contain an opt-out sentence and a company identity or address line | Needed in some jurisdictions; a text change goes through the skills | Business owner + legal | Decision and approved wording | Open |
| 8 | The content is limited to recap and recording: no offers, upsell or promotion | Mixed-purpose email changes the consent position | Business owner | Sign-off of the current skill rules | Open |
| 9 | Customer groups to exclude (refund or legal disputes, minors, enterprise contracts with special terms, complaints) | OD-11 covers sensitive topics inside a call; account-level exclusions need a list | Support lead | Exclusion rules or list | Open |
| 10 | Frequency limit (for example one email per call, at most one per customer per week) | Avoid repeated mail to the same customer | Business owner | Written limit | Open |
| 11 | The named approver for each C1 email and a backup | Individual approval needs a person | Business owner | Name and contact | Open |
| 12 | The named reply owner, response time and escalation path | See `docs/pilot-reply-monitoring.md` | Support lead | Name, SLA | Open |
| 13 | Retention of drafts, transcripts, the Send Log and approvals, and who may see them | `retention_period` is still undecided | Business owner / DPO | Retention decision | Open |
| 14 | The sender identity (display name, `supportcore@flexifunnels.com`) is acceptable to customers and replies are monitored | Customers will reply to that address | Business owner | Confirmation | Open |

## Rules for using this checklist
- C1 does not start until items 1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 11 and 12 are confirmed; items 2, 4, 7, 9, 10, 13 and 14 must be confirmed or accepted as risks in writing.
- The confirmation is recorded in this repository as a dated line with the person's name and no customer data.
- The agent never decides that a customer consents. A customer on any exclusion or suppression list is never contacted.
