# Brief for Legal: validate one jurisdiction first, so the C1 pilot can start sooner

For: Legal (via the Business Owner). Prepared 2026-11-02. No customer data. This is a request for Legal's validation, not legal advice; the agent does not decide any of this.

## Why this brief
The Business Owner's approval (2 Nov 2026) says Legal must map the applicable requirements for **all** countries with active customers before customer sending is enabled. Mapping every country takes time. The pilot (C1) is at most **5 emails**, each approved individually by a person. If Legal validates **one jurisdiction first**, C1 can use customers from that country only, and the other countries stay excluded until Legal has covered them. This needs the Business Owner's agreement because it narrows, but does not remove, the condition.

## What we ask Legal to confirm for the chosen jurisdiction
1. **Basis:** a post-call follow-up email (recap, next steps and a recording link for a call the customer had with FlexiFunnels support) is a service communication under that jurisdiction's rules. It contains no offers or promotion.
2. **Recording notice and permission:** what notice must be given before a call is recorded, what permission or consent is needed to **share the recording link with the customer by email**, and how this can be verified for a given call. (The agent cannot see this; a person attests per email.)
3. **Opt-out text:** whether the approved sentence is sufficient ("If you prefer not to receive further automated post-call follow-up emails, please reply to this email and let us know."), or whether a company identity or address line, an unsubscribe mechanism, or another statement is required.
4. **Link behaviour:** the emails go through Gmail, which rewrites each link to an address on `google.com` that shows a "Redirect Notice" page and carries an expiry of about 24 hours. The recording links open without login (approved conditionally by the Business Owner, only links relevant to the receiving customer). Is this acceptable?
5. **Retention:** the Business Owner set: drafts 90 days after the call, Send Log 12 months after sending, approval records 12 months, transcripts 30 days after processing, confidence records 90 days, suppression records as long as needed to honour the request. Do these fit that jurisdiction's requirements?
6. **Automated sending:** any rule about automated or templated email, a named sender, or a physical address that applies.

## What we need back
- The jurisdiction Legal has validated, the date, the person's name, and a short written statement (or a link to it) for the record in `docs/business-confirmations-record.md` (lines B1.1a and B1.2).
- Any condition Legal adds (for example a required sentence). A required wording change goes through the writing rules before C1.

## What stays closed
Nothing is sent, scheduled or enabled by this request. Customer sending, C1, C2 and C3 each need the user's written authorisation after the remaining conditions are met.
