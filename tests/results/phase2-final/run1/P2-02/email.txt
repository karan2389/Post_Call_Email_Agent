What we resolved:
- Following our guidance, you deleted the old A record at your domain registrar and added a CNAME record, using the target value from your FlexiFunnels domain settings, and your funnel now loads on your own domain.
- Following our guidance, you also deleted the second record for www and added a CNAME for www, and the www version now loads your funnel too.

What we covered:
- The old A record, which pointed to an old server address, was blocking the connection.
- The www version showed an error because a second record for www pointed somewhere else.
- The padlock for the secure certificate can take some time to appear after connecting.
