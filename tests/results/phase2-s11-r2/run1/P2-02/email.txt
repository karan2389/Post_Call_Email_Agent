What we resolved:
- Following our guidance, you deleted the old A record and added a CNAME record at your domain registrar, and the dashboard then showed your domain as connected and it loaded.
- Following our guidance, you also deleted a second record for the www version that pointed somewhere else and added a CNAME record for www with the same target value, and both the main domain and the www version now load your funnel.

What we covered:
- We explained that an old A record pointing to an old server address was blocking the connection, and that it needed to be replaced with a CNAME record using the target value shown in your FlexiFunnels domain settings.
- We also explained that the padlock for the secure certificate can take some time to appear after connecting. This is not yet resolved.
